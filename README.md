A few things I’m building:

- **Network diagnostics for macOS** — WinMTR’s macOS cousin, with a memory. Mark the moment the connection goes bad, reopen the session later, and export a report instead of trying to explain “it was broken five minutes ago.”
- **Android voice typing** — a floating mic, not another keyboard. Keep the keyboard you like, use a local speech engine, and find your dictation in the transcript history later.
- **Photo and video organizer** — a bit of detective work for files with missing dates or locations. It pieces together clues from travel records and GPS tracks, then proposes an organization plan. Review first, move second, with a way to undo changes.
- **ASL video library** — compare how different people sign the same word. Watch them side by side, slow the videos down, or loop the bit you missed.

I use AI coding tools to develop these projects, then test and iterate. They’re at different stages of development, and the source code is private.
